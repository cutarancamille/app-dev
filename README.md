# Alice in Borderland
> "In this world, you play... or you die."

## Summary
> **Alice in Borderland** is a japanese sci-fi thriller series that blends psychological drama,dystopian survival, and game-based suspense.
*Based on the manga by Haro Aso*, it follows Arisu, a video-game-obsessed young man who wakes up in an abandoned Tokyo where he must play deadly games to survive.

---

### Why I like it?
1. It **combines intense action with emotional depth**.
2. It explores **morality,teamwork, and survival instincts**.
3. The visuals and game concepts are **creative and haunting**.
